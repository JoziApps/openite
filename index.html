Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

How do I upload photos to the gallery?

Navigate to the Gallery section on this page, click the upload area, select your images, and add a comment. Your photos will appear instantly in the community gallery.

Can I browse activities without an account?

Yes! All activities are publicly viewable. You only need to interact (comment, upload, like) to participate in the community features.

How are activities verified?

Each activity includes a source link and has been reviewed by our team. Community feedback also helps keep information accurate and up-to-date.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. We use secure connections, respect your privacy, and offer anonymous submission options for sensitive feedback.

How can I suggest a new activity?

Use the Contact page to send us details, images, and sources for activities you'd like to see added. We review all submissions within 48 hours.
